About us

We are a well-established Nepalese and Indian restaurant in the heart of Southampton. Our Nepalese chefs cook traditional dishes in their own style, fresh every evening, with friendly service to match.

Our aim is simple: to serve Nepalese food at its most authentic and vibrant, just as you would find it in Nepal.

Bookings and celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, engagements or any celebration: we can seat parties of up to 50 in the restaurant, and we cater for events and special occasions. Call us to book.
